# Design State: Coolaboratory.com

_Last updated: 2026-08-09 by Lisa (discovery)_

## Brief
- **Problem:** The 2011 coolaboratory.com site is stale; Andrew needs a simple, single-page site that establishes credibility for his 1099 entity and routes interested visitors to milmoe.com.
- **Primary persona:** Recruiters/hiring managers/conference contacts verifying andrew@coolaboratory.com is a legitimate entity.
- **Success metric:** Andrew is comfortable using the domain/email professionally; visitors read it as legitimate and get routed to milmoe.com.
- **Brief document:** docs/designpowers/briefs/2026-08-09-coolaboratory-site.md

## Personas
- Recruiter/hiring manager — encountered andrew@coolaboratory.com at a conference or on a resume, checking it's real before taking it seriously.
- Conference contact — met Andrew in person, visiting the domain out of curiosity.

## Design Principles
1. Credibility over persuasion — this page proves legitimacy, it does not sell or convert.
2. Distinct from milmoe.com — maker-punk/DIY register, not clinical enterprise.
3. Radically simple — one page, no forms, no subpages, minimal decisions for the visitor.
4. Honest scope — plainly states it's not seeking contract work; no ambiguity.

## Taste Profile
- **Emotional target:** maker-punk, DIY, quietly credible
- **Quality level:** Production (small scope, but should feel finished, not a placeholder)
- **Key references:** 2009 Coolaboratory manifesto (voice); existing logo (palette anchor — deep purple, warm rust/brown)
- **Aesthetic principles:** "If you spend the time doing it, do it right" — restrained craft, not corporate polish
- **Taste document:** none formal — early signal captured in brief

No formal taste calibration run — task scope (single static page) didn't warrant full `design-taste` pass. Craft evaluation uses brief's early signal + general quality standards.
